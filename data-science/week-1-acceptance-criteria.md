# Week 1 Acceptance Criteria

#### Code & Structure

- The notebook executes sequentially from top to bottom without errors.
- Code cells include meaningful comments.
- Markdown headers separate each phase and Markdown cells are used to document code and insights.

#### Part 1

A market summary is present and includes:
- Kaufland's market position relative to key local competitors.
- At least 3 operational or regulatory differences compared to Germany.
- A list of Czech public holidays with mandatory store closures.
- A list of at least 2 local cultural events or seasonal habits that could increase demand for specific fruits or vegetables.

#### Part 2

- The notebook successfully outputs a single, consolidated dataset and saves it as a `.parquet` file.
- Markdown cells document how raw tables were joined and preprocessed (missing values, cleaning, reshaping).
- Any data quality decisions or trade-offs are explained and justified.

#### Part 3

- The notebook contains an EDA section that investigates patterns across the data.
- The EDA section documents at least 3 distinct data insights.
- At least 2 presentation-ready visualizations are included (i.e., they include clear titles, labeled axes, and readable scales).

#### Bonus

- At least one data anomaly or outlier in the visual EDA is explicitly explained using the market research.
